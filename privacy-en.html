Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Math Practice handles browser-stored practice records and how cookies may be used if Google AdSense advertising is enabled in the future.

Information We Collect

This site does not require sign-in and does not actively ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, ages, or other personal information. Daily practice sessions, question counts, and correct answer counts are stored in the user's own browser localStorage to show the daily practice summary.

Children's Privacy

This site is designed for children to practice math. It does not include comments, account registration, or features that intentionally collect children's personal information. Parents or teachers may use the site together with children.

Google AdSense and Cookies

If this site displays Google AdSense ads in the future, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users may also visit aboutads.info to learn about choices for third-party vendor cookies.

Data Sharing

This site does not sell personal information. If Google AdSense or other third-party services are used, those services may process necessary technical data, such as cookies, IP addresses, device information, or browser information, according to their own privacy policies.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when site features or advertising services change. Updates will be posted on this page.
