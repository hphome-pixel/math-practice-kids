Contact

If you have feedback about question design, the interface, or mini games, you can use this page as the contact and feedback reference.

Feedback Topics

  • Questions feel too easy or too difficult.
  • Handwriting recognition does not match the expected digit.
  • You would like to suggest another practice mode or mini game.
  • The layout looks incorrect on a phone or tablet.

Contact Method

For now, please use GitHub Issues or the contact method provided by the site owner. If a dedicated email address is added later, it will be listed on this page.

Privacy Reminder

To protect children's privacy, please do not include a child's name, school, address, phone number, or other personally identifying information in feedback.
